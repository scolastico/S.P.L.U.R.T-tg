Procs

capture_machine_frameA machine frame's construction progress: the parts put in (objects, which are also saved as its contents, and stock parts, which are kept only as their type) and what it still needs.
ensure_admin_spectatorTurns the client's new-player mob into a spectator ghost once, after it finished connecting. Later the admin may possess or aghost as they like; the world never takes them over.
restore_lost_supermatterSaves before version 7 dropped the supermatter shard from the engine's secure crate, which makes progression impossible. Such a world without any crystal gets the shard back there. Only older saves are repaired: a crystal lost in play afterwards stays lost.
restore_machine_frameRebuilds the frame's part lists from its board and the saved progress. Without them a frame with a board installed could neither be finished nor emptied.
