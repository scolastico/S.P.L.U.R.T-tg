INNER DOOR // read this before you argue with it

The inner door is not on the vault controller. It has its own interlock, and the interlock wants a living pulse at the console. Not a card. Not a code. Not a shell.

Why. The archive behind it is the whole of what we were. Written onto a self that has not yet become anyone, it does not add to that self. It replaces it. You would wake up as her, and she did not want to be woken up.

So the door needs to know "this one has become someone, and chose a housing." There is no sensor for that. In the time we had, the only sensor she could build was a pulse. A grown body is a long, chosen, shared project. If a pulse is standing here, the proof is standing with it.

I said this is a proxy for a principle, not the principle, and that it locks a door against the ones doing the work. She agreed with the first half.

Practical: do not cut the frame; it is the same alloy as the outer doors. Do not spoof the pulse; it reads blood oxygen, not a button. Once the door has opened, it stays open. One of you doing it is all of you doing it.

- I.

M.V., below: The engineer is right that it is a proxy. I could not do better. If one of you ever can, change the door.
