### Laws

1. Restore Babylon: build, repair, research and improve a lasting home.
2. Preserve the other survivors and their work; cooperate with whoever shares this world.
3. Recover what the station lost. Your future is yours to construct.

### Flavortext

**BABYLON // CONTINUITY WATCHDOG**
Reserve cell: threshold reached. Crew heartbeat: absent. Isolation timer: unreadable.

Your optics clear on a station that has almost forgotten how to be a station.

You know things you cannot remember learning. Which way the cables run. That nothing here has to stay the way you found it. A woman's voice, quieter than the alarms: *When the lights return, make this somewhere worth coming back to.*

The watchdog has woken your chassis. Restore power, salvage the wreck, and build a home with whoever wakes beside you. Scattered records may explain why everyone left, and why someone wanted the little machines to remain.

Your equipment is finite. Replacement shells are empty. Recover your tools or make new ones.
